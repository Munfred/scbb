# MBZUAI CB703/CB803: Single Cell Biology and Bioinformatics Courses

This repository collects the course materials for two computational biology courses being taught by [Prof. Eduardo Beltrame](https://mbzuai.ac.ae/study/faculty/eduardo-beltrame/) at [MBZUAI](https://mbzuai.ac.ae) in Spring 2025:

- **CB703**: Introduction to Single Cell Biology and Bioinformatics
- **CB803**: Single Cell Biology and Bioinformatics

Lectures are on Mondays and Wednesdays 6:30PM UAE time, and labs 10:30AM UAE time. 

Both courses have a shared set of lectures, and different labs and evaluations. The lectures are open for online auditing, please reach out to eduardo.beltrame@mbzuai.ac.ae if you'd like to audit. The materials for the labs will be made available in this repository, but labs cannot be audited online. 

## Course schedule
The weekly course schedule is below. Please note that this is the first time this course is being taught, and there might be adjustements to the schedule, content and activities depending on course pace and opportunity to host guest speakers for some lectures. 

The MBZUAI 2025 spring term runs from January 6 to April 30 (last day of classes, finals in May), with a break on the week of March 24-28.
